Questions

Common questions

What does the qualification process involve?
The $10,000 strategic diagnostic is a deep-dive analysis of your current operating model, technology stack, and market position. We assess readiness for AI-native transformation and deliver a roadmap. If both sides agree to proceed, the fee rolls into the engagement.
How long does a full engagement take?
Timeline depends on scope, but most full transformations span 6–12 months. The first 90 days focus on architecture and agent deployment. The remainder is about compounding — making the system smarter every cycle.
Do you work with companies outside tech?
Yes. AI-native transformation is industry-agnostic. We've worked with fintech, healthtech, logistics, and professional services. The common thread is leadership willing to rebuild the operating model — not just add AI on top.
What happens if we're not ready after the diagnostic?
We'll tell you honestly. Not every company is ready to become the ONE. In that case, we'll provide a clear roadmap of what needs to change before a full engagement makes sense — no pressure, no sales push.
